# KizuBot — brand & content brief for the shorts

## What KizuBot is (one paragraph)
KizuBot is an AI automation bot for **PokeXGames (PxG)**, the Brazilian Pokémon-themed MMORPG. It runs the player's character on **cloud machines 24/7**, so the player can turn the PC off, and it does the grind: hunts with cavebot routes and AI combat, catches (with shiny detection), farms resources, fishes, heals, and sends **real-time alarms to WhatsApp/Telegram** when something matters (shiny, mega, boss, rare drop, capture). The player follows and controls everything from a web panel, including on the phone. Site: **kizubot.com**. Official channels: TikTok, YouTube and Instagram **@kizubotpxg**.

## The 13 modules (from kizubot.com/llms.txt)
Cavebot (waypoint routes, recorder) · Combat (targeting, lure, stun, loot, positioning) · Healing (revive, potions, berries, Smart Swap, keep distance) · Catch (pokeball choice, catch all, shiny catching) · Farming (resources, minigames, headbutt, professions) · Fishing (modes, hook timing) · Alarms (WhatsApp/Telegram for game events) · Party · Engine · Actions (custom rules: 50+ conditions, 30+ actions) · Account · Setup Pokémon (skill categories) · Humanization (threshold variance, micro-pauses, hunt breaks).

## Claims you must NOT make (even though some appear on the site)
- **No "garantia" as a money-back promise.** The site shows a "Garantia de 30 dias" badge, but the refund policy (kizubot.com/refunds) is a strict no-refund policy for dissatisfaction. Do not build a short around the guarantee and never imply refunds. If you mention it at all, only as the plain words "garantia de 30 dias" in small print — better to leave it out.
- **No safety/ban promises:** no "100% seguro", "sem ban", "indetectável", "anti-ban", "GM não pega". The refund page itself warns that any bot carries ban risk. Don't feature GM/staff evasion, "dance for GM", verification bypass, or the "nunca caiu em mass ban" line.
- No real-money earnings (R$/US$/€), no "renda extra", no RMT. In-game currency from real prints (kk, dl) is fine.
- No invented numbers: no drop rates, shiny odds, hours-to-shiny, rankings, poll percentages, follower counts.
- Don't name or mock competitor bots, the game's staff, or PxG itself.
- **No scarcity:** the site's "OFERTA LIMITADA - ÚLTIMAS VAGAS!" badge is permanent with no real counter → no "vagas", "últimas vagas", countdowns or waitlist claims. No "4.8 estrelas / 500 avaliações" (no review system behind it).
- **No PxG staff, ever.** Some Discord prints on the site include game staff (per the official PxG wiki staff page). Never use prints with them (ASSETS.md excludes them).
- **The panel screenshots on the site show an old UI.** Don't present them as the current panel. To show "the panel", design a generic brand-styled mock and don't call it a screenshot.
- Quote the images, never the site's testimonial captions (several captions don't match their screenshots).

## Audience
Adult (the terms require 18+) Brazilian PxG players, mostly men 18–35, many ex-Tibia players, many with jobs or school, who grind for hours (levels, resources/"mato", bosses, megas, shinies) and fear missing a rare spawn while asleep or away. Pains: the grind eats sleep/work/family time; missing a shiny "por 1 segundo"; repetitive clicking; megas and shinies that take months by hand. Desires: progress without sacrificing life, the thrill of the shiny alert, flexing captures to friends, belonging to the PxG tribe. Objections: "PC precisa ficar ligado?" (no), "é difícil de configurar?" (customers say it's easy; support helps; guided panel), "dá pra ver do celular?" (yes), price.

## What already works on the official channels (research, Oct 2026)
YouTube @kizubotpxg: 82 subscribers, 253 videos, median ≈ 70 views on the last 48 Shorts (max 277). **Titles about time away and the night did best; generic "Cena real:" titles flopped.** TikTok @kizubotpxg: 25 followers. Competitors sell local macros with "FIQUE RICO" pitches — KizuBot owns a different, more credible angle: **cloud / PC desligado, alarme no WhatsApp, tempo de volta pra sua vida**. Lead with outcomes; "IA" is a secondary proof point (some Brazilian gamers distrust "IA").

## Voice
Talks like a PxG player talking to a friend: short, punchy, a bit of humor and "zoeira", pt-BR informal ("você", "tá", "pra", "kkk" only inside real prints). Confident, never arrogant. Never corporate. Use second person ("seu char", "você dorme"). Exclamation marks sparingly; let the visuals shout.

## Visual identity
Dark near-black aubergine (#0b0712) + violet (#a855f7) + lavender (#d8b4fe) + warm white (#f5f0fa), a tiny warm-yellow or lime accent. The mascot: a seated gunmetal robot with one glowing purple eye, a laptop and a purple chair — the eye glow is the signature. Families may use their own palette (horror, blueprint, newsprint, pop), but every short ends on the KizuBot end card: logo + eye glow + **kizubot.com** + the sonic logo.

## The end card (every short)
Logo pop (with eye pulse) on the `logo` sound, then `kizubot.com`, then ONE ask: a comment prompt tied to the story (best for reach) or "link na bio". Keep it ≥ 2.5 s. Optionally finish with a loop bridge into the first frame.
