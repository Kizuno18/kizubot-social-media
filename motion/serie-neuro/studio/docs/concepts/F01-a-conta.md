# F01 — A Conta (041–045)

**Lever:** loss aversion (losses weigh ~2× gains), anchoring and price framing, concrete numbers. The viewer does the math with you and feels the loss before the fix arrives.
**Look:** dark "calculator/receipt" UI on the brand background; big tabular numbers; red (#ef4444) for what is lost, lime (#c6ff3d) for what is gained; count-ups with the `counter` SFX, a tape-stop or hard stop right before the worst number, an impact + red flash when it lands. Each episode gets its own prop (receipt, calendar, coin stack, click counter, weekend planner) so the series does not repeat itself.
**Music:** phonk or trap. **Golden reference:** 041 is done — study it, then make 042–045 better.

### 042 · seu-expediente — Seu char trabalha menos que você
- **Hook (0–1 s):** "SEU CHAR TRABALHA MENOS QUE VOCÊ." (slam, a time-clock "punch card" stamps in).
- **Beats:** a month calendar (30 cells). Work days: 8 h × 22 = 176 h → cells fill gray "parado". Sleep: 8 h × 30 = 240 h → more gray. Total 416 h of 720 h = **58% do mês com o char parado** (count-up, red). Re-hook at ~6 s: "E o resto? Você jogando cansado." Then a purple sweep: every cell turns purple "farmando" — "24h por dia, 30 dias" (720 h). Claims: 100% AFK, uso com PC desligado, acesso pelo celular.
- **Peak:** the full purple calendar + "720h/mês".
- **End:** logo; ask "Qual sua profissão?".
- **Assets:** brand only (optional: `hud-8434-loots.png` safe crop as proof "rodando desde cedo").
- **Pinned comment:** "Seu char trabalha mais ou menos que você? 😅"

### 043 · preco-de-um-lanche — R$ 250 é caro?
- **Hook:** "R$ 250 POR MÊS É CARO?" (price tag swings in on a string).
- **Beats:** reframe step by step with a big divider animation: R$ 250 ÷ 30 dias = **R$ 8,33/dia** ("menos que um lanche"), ÷ 24 h = **R$ 0,35 por hora de farm** (250 ÷ 720 = 0,347). Re-hook: "Quanto vale 1 hora do SEU tempo?" — a clock with a price ticker. Contrast: "1 hora sua vs 1 hora do bot" (you: sleep, work, family; bot: hunt, catch, fish). Close: "planos a partir de R$ 250/mês".
- **Peak:** "R$ 0,35/hora" slam in lime.
- **Assets:** brand; optional real quote `queria-ter-comprado-antes.png` ("eu queria mt ter comprado isso antes") as the last proof beat.
- **Pinned:** "Quanto vale 1 hora do seu tempo?"
- **Note:** never compare with a specific product price; "menos que um lanche" is enough.
