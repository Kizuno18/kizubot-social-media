# F04 — Espera o Alarme (056–060)

**Lever:** anticipation. Dopamine spikes on the *expectation* of a reward (reward prediction error) — a buzzing phone with a hidden message, a countdown, a blur that slowly sharpens. The payoff is a real customer alarm/capture. The KizuBot `alert` sound (the brand motif twice) plays on every reveal: repeated across the series, viewers learn "this sound = reward" (classical conditioning, sonic branding).
**Look:** dark, cinematic, close-up phone on a table or in a hand; the notification preview is blurred/pixelated ("1 nova mensagem"), a countdown ring or "ADIVINHA O QUE ERA?" prompt; the reveal is a blur-to-sharp + light burst + the real print in a card tagged "print real de cliente". Each episode opens at the real timestamp from the print (16:42, 13:36…). Vary the setting: table, bedside, pocket, wrist, desk.
**Music:** tension (96) for the build (heartbeat, clock ticks), `stops` silence for 0.4 s right before the reveal, then a drop (phonk/house section) on the reveal.
