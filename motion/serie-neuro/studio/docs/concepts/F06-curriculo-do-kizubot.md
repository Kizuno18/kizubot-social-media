# F06 — Currículo do KizuBot (066–070)

**Lever:** humor through personification (benign violation: a bot treated as a job candidate), plus anchoring ("pretensão salarial: R$ 250/mês" next to "disponibilidade 24/7") and authority (experience = 170+ updates, 13 skills). Office humor is universally relatable in Brazil (CLT, RH, entrevista).
**Look:** paper documents on a desk: CV sheet, HR interview card, LinkedIn-ish profile (generic, no LinkedIn logo), performance review form, recommendation letters, a rubber stamp "CONTRATADO" / "APROVADO". Typewriter (Special Elite) for forms, Inter for UI, red stamp ink. The robot head photo-clipped onto the CV like a 3×4 photo.
**Music:** lofi (84) or gameshow (122); `type`/`type_long` for typing, `shutter` for the 3×4 photo, `impact` + `right` on stamps.
