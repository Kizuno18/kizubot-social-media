# F14 — Prova Real (106–110)

**Lever:** social proof and emotional contagion. One real customer, one strong line, made loud. Short (15–18 s): hook with the quote's most surprising words, show the print, zoom on the line, react, end card. People trust peers over brands, and a real chat screenshot reads as unscripted.
**Look:** Pop Bold — flat lime #c6ff3d / purple #8a3cff / pink #ff4fd8 / #111 blocks, 4 px black outlines, hard 8 px offset shadows, rotating star/circle stickers, Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold. The print sits in a white card with a "PRINT REAL" sticker; the key line gets a highlighter sweep and a zoom. Rotate the dominant color per episode.
**Music:** funk (130) or phonk; `pop` on stickers, `snap` on zooms, `success` on the highlighter.
