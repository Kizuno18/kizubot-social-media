# F16 — Speedrun (116–120)

**Lever:** time pressure and a ticking timer hold attention (the viewer waits for the clock to stop), chunking makes the product easy to remember, and the short length (12–17 s) earns rewatches and loops.
**Look:** a speedrun HUD: huge monospace timer (JetBrains Mono / Rajdhani), a splits panel (split name · time · delta in green/gold/red), "PB" and "WR!" badges, a category title card ("KizuBot% Any%"). Fast cuts on the beat. Gold flash on a personal best. Generic design — don't copy LiveSplit's look exactly.
**Music:** chiptune (150) or trap (145); the timer ticks on the beat; `levelup`/`coin` on gold splits.

### 116 · kizubot-em-10s — KizuBot em 10 segundos
- **Hook:** "KIZUBOT EM 10 SEGUNDOS. VALENDO." + "3, 2, 1, GO".
- **Splits:** "Configura o bot ✓" → "Grava a rota ✓" → "Liga e desliga o PC ✓" → "Vai dormir ✓" → "Alarme de shiny no WhatsApp ✓" (alert sound) — each with a split time; the timer stops at 9.9 s → "WR!".
- **Peak:** "WR" gold explosion. End card. Loop bridge: end on "3, 2, 1".
- **Claims:** uso com PC desligado, alarmes no WhatsApp, detecção de shiny. **Assets:** brand; optional `shiny-cubone.png` flash at the alarm split.
- **Pinned:** "Bate esse tempo aí 😎"

### 117 · 13-modulos — 13 módulos em 13 segundos
- **Hook:** "13 MÓDULOS. 13 SEGUNDOS." (timer 0:13 counting down).
- **Beats:** one module per second, each a split with an icon (simple SVG) and 2–4 words: Cavebot "anda sozinho" · Combat "luta com IA" · Healing "cura e revive" · Catch "captura" · Farming "coleta recursos" · Fishing "pesca no AFK" · Alarms "avisa no WhatsApp" · Party · Engine · Actions "50+ condições" · Account · Setup Pokémon · Humanização "ritmo humano".
- **Peak:** the 13 icons collapse into the robot. "Tudo num bot só."
- **Pinned:** "Qual módulo você mais usaria?"

### 118 · do-zero-ao-farm — Do zero ao farm em 3 passos

- **Splits:** PHASE_01 "Configure seu bot" (a generic brand-styled panel mock — the site's panel screenshots are an old UI, never present them as current) → PHASE_02 "Defina os waypoints" (route drawing over a map grid) → PHASE_03 "Ligue e acompanhe pelo celular" (phone with alarm).
