# F17 — Infomercial (121–125)

**Lever:** value stacking ("mas espera, tem mais!"), anchoring, and nostalgia for 90s/2000s Brazilian TV shopping — a parody everyone recognises, so the ad becomes the joke and people share it. Black-and-white "problem" scenes → color "solution" is a classic contrast device.
**Look:** 4:3 TV feel inside the 9:16 frame (rounded CRT screen, scanlines `.kz-scan`, slight chroma offset), starbursts "NOVO!", "OFERTA!", yellow/red/blue palette, chunky italic type (Anton/Bangers), "AO VIVO" bug, phone banner "LINK NA BIO" instead of a 0800 number. Problem scenes desaturated + `vinyl` crackle; the product in full color.
**Music:** gameshow (122); `ding`, `coin`, `swoosh_hit`, `impact`; a `drumroll` before the price.
**Rules:** no invented "valor total", no fake countdown timers, no "últimas unidades/vagas", no scarcity of any kind.

---

### 121 · cansado-de-farmar — Cansado de farmar na mão?
- **Hook:** "CANSADO DE FARMAR NA MÃO?" (B&W: a tired player silhouette, eyes closing, click counter).
- **Beats:** "SEUS PROBLEMAS ACABARAM!" color burst → KizuBot robot spins in on a turntable → "100% AFK!" → "Caça! Captura! Pesca!" (each a starburst) → "Avisa no WhatsApp!" (`alert`) → "Mas espera…" → "funciona com o PC desligado!" → price card "planos a partir de R$ 250/mês" → end card.
- **Pinned:** "Quem lembra dos comerciais da madrugada? 📺"

### 122 · mas-espera-tem-mais — Mas espera… tem mais!
- **Hook:** "MAS ESPERA… TEM MAIS!" (repeated as a running gag).
- **Beats:** a value stack tower: each module block drops onto the pile with "E TEM MAIS!" — Cavebot, Combate, Cura, Captura, Farming, Pesca, Alarmes, Party, Actions (50+ condições), Humanização… the tower gets comically tall, the camera tilts up. Final: "Tudo isso num bot só — a partir de R$ 250/mês (menos de R$ 9 por dia)."
- **Pinned:** "Qual foi o melhor 'tem mais'? 😂"

### 123 · demonstracao-ao-vivo — Demonstração ao vivo!
- **Hook:** "DEMONSTRAÇÃO AO VIVO!" (TV with "AO VIVO" bug).
- **Beats:** the real gameplay clip (`kit/video/rypherior.mp4` or `tangrowth.mp4`, use the safe version from ASSETS.md) plays inside the CRT, presenter captions point at it: "Olha ele caçando sozinho!", "Ninguém no teclado!", "Usando os golpes certinho!". Lower third: "gameplay real do KizuBot". Then "E se aparecer shiny? Ele te avisa!" (`alert` + alarm line).
- **Pinned:** "Quer ver mais gameplay? 👀"

### 124 · compare — Compare!
- **Hook:** "COMPARE!" (the classic infomercial comparison board slides in).
- **Beats:** two columns "FARM NA MÃO" (B&W, sad trombone `wrong`) vs "KIZUBOT" (color, `right`): "Funciona com o PC desligado" ✗ / ✓ · "Farma enquanto você dorme" ✗ / ✓ · "Te avisa no WhatsApp" ✗ / ✓ · "Detecta shiny, mega e boss" ✗ / ✓ · "Pesca no AFK" ✗ / ✓ · "Acompanha pelo celular" ✗ / ✓. Each row stamps in on the beat. Then the presenter caption: "E o preço?" → drumroll → "menos de R$ 9 por dia" (planos a partir de R$ 250/mês).
- **Rule:** compare only with playing by hand — never with other bots or brands.
- **Pinned:** "Qual linha pesou mais pra você? 🤔"
