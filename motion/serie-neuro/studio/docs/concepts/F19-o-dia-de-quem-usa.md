# F19 — O Dia de Quem Usa (131–135)

**Lever:** aspiration and future pacing (the viewer imagines their own day with the product), narrative arc across a day, social proof via real prints at each timestamp.
**Look:** vlog / day-in-the-life: a big timestamp per scene (Space Grotesk or Rajdhani, "06:30"), a "● REC" camera frame, handwritten annotations (Caveat) with arrows, light that shifts with the hour (dawn peach → noon white → dusk orange → night violet). Scenes are flat illustrations + real prints in cards.
**Music:** lofi (84) or house (124); `shutter` on scene changes, `notif`/`alert` on alarms.
