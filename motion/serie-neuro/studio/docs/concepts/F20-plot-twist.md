The twist is the peak, so the peak-end rule makes the whole short feel bigger. Each is a true story told from one real customer print.
**Look:** cinematic: 2.39:1 letterbox bars (the story lives in the band; captions sit just above the bottom bar, still inside the safe box), film grain, slow push-ins and parallax, chapter cards ("CAPÍTULO 1 — A SAÍDA"), elegant serif subtitles (Cormorant Garamond / Cinzel for titles), a warm grade that turns cool or bright at the twist. **Music:** epic (90): low strings and braams; silence before the twist; impact + drop on the reveal.
**Rule:** the twist must be literally what the print says — no embellishment.
